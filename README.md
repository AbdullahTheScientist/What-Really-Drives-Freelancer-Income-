# 💼💸 What Really Drives Freelancer Income?

A data-driven exploratory analysis to understand the key factors influencing **freelancer income**, including skills, experience, region, education, and freelancing platforms.

This project uses real-world data to uncover patterns in **hourly rates** and **annual earnings**, supported by clear visualizations and evidence-based insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Freelancing has become a global career path, but income varies significantly across individuals.  
This project aims to answer a central question:

> **What really drives freelancer income?**

Through exploratory data analysis (EDA), we investigate how different factors contribute to income differences among freelancers worldwide.

---

## 📊 Dataset

- **Source:** Kaggle  
- **Dataset Name:** Freelancer Income vs Skills  
- **Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shaistashahid/freelancer-income-vs-skills

The dataset includes information such as:
- Freelancer category  
- Primary skills  
- Years of experience & experience level  
- Region and country  
- Education background  
- Hourly rate and annual income  
- Primary freelancing platform  

---

## 🔍 Key Questions Answered

- Which freelancer categories earn the highest average income?
- How does experience level impact hourly rate and annual income?
- Is there a relationship between years of experience and earnings?
- Which skills are associated with higher pay?
- How does income vary across regions and countries?
- Do education levels influence earning potential?
- Which freelancing platforms generate higher income?
- Do direct clients pay more than freelancing marketplaces?

## 🎯 Key Insights

- Senior freelancers generally earn significantly more than junior freelancers.
- Technical roles such as AI/ML and DevOps tend to have higher income potential.
- Freelancers working with direct clients often earn more than those using marketplaces.
- Income varies noticeably across regions and countries.
- Higher hourly rates do not always guarantee higher annual income.
